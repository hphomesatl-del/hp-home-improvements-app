# 21-Phase Renovation Workflow

Standard kitchen renovation timeline for HP Home Improvements projects. Based on proven methodology from the Martin Renovation project timeline.

## Timeline Overview

**Total Duration:** ~60-70 days (typical)

| Phase | Task | Contractor | Duration | Start | Finish | Materials Needed |

## Parallel Work Opportunities

Some phases can overlap to accelerate the timeline:

- **Plumbing Rough → HVAC:** Can start while plumbing is finishing
- **Drywall → Electrical/Plumbing Final Prep:** Electrical and plumbing can be ready for final installation while drywall is being finished
- **Paint → Flooring:** Can happen in parallel with careful scheduling

## Critical Path

The critical path (phases that delay the entire project if delayed):
1. Demolition → Framing → Drywall → Paint → Flooring (longest sequential chain)

## Contractor Notes

- **Fidel (Carpentry):** Handles demo, framing, insulation, trim — high-frequency contact
- **Jorge (Drywall):** Critical quality gatekeeper — determines smoothness for paint
- **Jose (Painter):** Quality of prep work from Jorge directly impacts final finish
- **Hector (Plumbing/HVAC):** Two separate rough-ins; coordinates with framing
- **Axel (Electrical):** Two phases; rough-in early, finals after other trades complete
- **Andres (Tile):** Backsplash after cabinets/countertops installed
- **Luciano (Flooring):** Almost last phase — must follow paint completion
- **Daniel Wheeler (Cabinets):** Custom timeline — should be ordered early, installed after paint

## Quality Checkpoints

- **End of Framing:** Verify structural integrity and openings
- **End of Rough Electrical/Plumbing/HVAC:** Inspections required before covering
- **End of Drywall:** Surface quality inspection before paint
- **End of Paint:** Trim and fixture readiness for finals
- **End of Flooring:** Final walkthrough before punch-out
