# Customer Decision Checklist

Guide customers through critical selections required during their renovation project. Each decision is tied to specific contractor phases and has deadlines to avoid delays.

## Overview

Customers must make 7 major decisions during the renovation process. Each decision has a deadline tied to contractor schedules to avoid project delays.

## Decision Checklist

### 1. 🚿 Plumbing Fixtures
**Deadline:** Before Plumbing Rough Phase (Week 2-3)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Toilet(s) — make, model, style
- [ ] Faucet(s) — kitchen, bathroom sinks, shower, tub
- [ ] Sink(s) — kitchen, bathroom vanity
- [ ] Tub/Shower — material, dimensions, fixtures
- [ ] Water heater — size, type, fuel
- [ ] Drain fixtures — style consistency across kitchen/bath

**Notes:**
- Orders must be placed before rough-in phase begins
- Lead times vary; order early if custom/special finishes
- Rough-in requires knowing exact fixture locations
- All selections should match overall design aesthetic

**Contractor:** Hector (Plumbing/HVAC)

---

### 2. ⚡ Electrical Fixtures
**Deadline:** Before Electrical Rough Phase (Week 2-3)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Light fixtures — recessed, under-cabinet, pendant, ceiling
- [ ] Receptacles (outlets) — standard, USB, specialty
- [ ] Switches — style, color, dimmer vs. standard
- [ ] Can lights — quantity, trim finish, color temperature
- [ ] Appliance rough-in — locations for dishwasher, disposal, microwave
- [ ] Special circuits — charger outlets, wine fridge, etc.

**Notes:**
- Rough-in requires exact locations and quantities
- Under-cabinet lighting must be coordinated with cabinet design
- Can light placement affects ceiling aesthetics
- Consider future outlets for emerging tech

**Contractor:** Axel (Electrical)

---

### 3. 🗄️ Cabinets
**Deadline:** Before Framing/Demolition (Week 1) — **EARLIEST DECISION**

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Cabinet style — shaker, traditional, modern, transitional
- [ ] Material — plywood, solid wood, veneer
- [ ] Finish — paint color, stain, glaze
- [ ] Kitchen layout — configuration, island? peninsula?
- [ ] Vanity cabinets — bathroom style match?
- [ ] Quality level — HP supplied, custom, specialty contractor
- [ ] Hardware — knobs, pulls, soft-close hinges
- [ ] Island specs (if applicable) — seating, overhang dimensions

**Notes:**
- This is the longest lead time (custom cabinets = 6-8 weeks)
- Must be ordered before demolition begins
- Design phase should happen during proposal stage
- Cabinet placement dictates framing layout
- Backsplash tile is contingent on cabinet height/placement

**Contractor:** Daniel Wheeler (Wheeler Woodworks) or supplier

**Timeline:** Order in Week 1, delivery target Day 30-45

---

### 4. 🪨 Countertops
**Deadline:** Before Cabinet Installation (Week 4-5)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Material — quartz, granite, laminate, butcher block, concrete
- [ ] Color/Pattern — sample approval
- [ ] Edge profile — bullnose, ogee, waterfall, etc.
- [ ] Backsplash material — coordinate with countertop
- [ ] Thickness — 3/4" or 1.5"
- [ ] Sink opening — undermount or drop-in
- [ ] Cooktop cutout — dimensions and specifications
- [ ] Fabricator — price quotes and timeline

**Notes:**
- 2-3 week lead time for custom fabrication
- Must be templated after cabinets are installed
- Material samples should be viewed in natural light
- Backsplash tile selection should complement countertop

**Contractor:** Specialty Contractor (TBD by customer/designer)

**Timeline:** Selection by Day 25, template & order by Day 35, delivery Day 50

---

### 5. ✨ Trim Selection
**Deadline:** Before Trim & Millwork Phase (Week 4)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Baseboards — height, profile, material
- [ ] Crown molding — profile, style, color to match cabinets
- [ ] Door frames — style match, finish, hardware
- [ ] Door style — solid, hollow, panel configuration
- [ ] Paint color for trim — match cabinets or different?
- [ ] Quarter round/shoe molding — if applicable

**Notes:**
- Trim installation follows paint in most cases
- Crown molding should complement cabinet style
- Door selection affects overall kitchen cohesion
- Finish color affects perception of space (light = larger, dark = dramatic)

**Contractor:** Fidel (Carpentry)

---

### 6. 🎨 Tile Selections
**Deadline:** Before Flooring & Backsplash Phases (Week 3-4)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] **Floor tile** — material, size, pattern, color
  - Recommend ceramic or porcelain for kitchens (durable)
  - Smaller tiles on shower floors (less slippery)
- [ ] **Wall tile** (if applicable) — color, size
- [ ] **Backsplash tile** — pattern, size, grout color, sealer
  - Coordinate with countertop color
  - Consider maintenance (darker grout hides stains)
- [ ] **Grout color** — light, medium, dark
- [ ] **Trim pieces** — bullnose, corner trim, edge pieces
- [ ] **Sealer** — standard or high-performance (near sink/stove)

**Notes:**
- Order early; custom tiles = long lead times
- Grout color dramatically affects tile appearance
- Sample grout joint on your tile before full install
- Light-colored grout requires more maintenance
- Backsplash can be simple (subway) or elaborate (mosaic)

**Contractor:** Andres (Tile)

**Timeline:** Selection by Day 20, order by Day 25, installation Day 45-50

---

### 7. 🎨 Paint Colors
**Deadline:** Before Paint Phase (Week 4-5)

**Required Selections:**
- [ ] Wall color(s) — kitchen, adjacent rooms
- [ ] Ceiling color — white, or tinted?
- [ ] Trim color — match walls, white, or accent?
- [ ] Cabinet color (if painting) — finish type, sheen
- [ ] Paint brand — Sherwin Williams recommended
- [ ] Sheen — matte, eggshell, satin, semi-gloss, gloss
  - Kitchens: Satin or semi-gloss (easier to clean)
  - High-traffic areas: Satin+ (more durable)
  - Ceilings: Matte or eggshell

**Notes:**
- Always view paint samples in actual room lighting
- Paint samples change dramatically at different times of day
- Lighting can shift color perception significantly
- Cabinet finish paint choice depends on traffic/use
- Sherwin Williams offers excellent customer service and warranty
- Sheen level affects durability and light reflection

**Contractor:** Jose (Painter)

**Timeline:** Selection by Day 30, paint day ~Day 35

## Communication Strategy

- **Week 1:** Present cabinet options and get initial approval
- **Week 2:** Present plumbing and electrical fixture options
- **Week 3:** Confirm paint colors, tile selections, trim styles
- **Week 4:** Present final countertop samples, approve fabricator
- **Week 5:** Final walkthrough before installation phases
